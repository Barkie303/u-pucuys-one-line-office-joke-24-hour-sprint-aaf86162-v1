Entry for the make.fun bounty **One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint** ($2 pool, top 8 paid).

`main.ts` writes a single one-line joke about office life to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The meeting could have been an email, the email could have been a Slack message, and the Slack message could have been silence.

The joke escalates down the chain of office communication — meeting, email, Slack — and lands on the punchline that none of it needed to exist at all.
